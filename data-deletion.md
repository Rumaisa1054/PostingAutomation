# User Data Deletion Instructions

Users of PostingAutomation can request deletion of their personal information and associated application data.

To request deletion:

1. Send an email to dev.rumaisa@gmail.com
2. Use the subject: Data Deletion Request.
3. Include your Facebook account name and explain that you are requesting deletion of data associated with Re_tail's pages
We will review your request and delete applicable stored information within 30 days, subject to legal and security requirements.

You may also revoke the application's access through your Facebook account settings.

For questions, contact dev.rumaisa@gmail.com
