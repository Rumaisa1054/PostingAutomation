# User Data Deletion Instructions

Users of **Re_tail (PostingAutomation)** can request deletion of their personal information and associated application data collected through Facebook and Pinterest integrations.

## How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion:

1. Send an email to **dev.rumaisa@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject: **Data Deletion Request**.
3. Identify the platform involved (Facebook, Pinterest, or both).
4. Include your Facebook account name or Pinterest username, as applicable.
5. Explain that you are requesting deletion of data associated with Re_tail.

We will review your request and delete applicable stored information within **30 days**, subject to legal and security requirements.

## Revoking Application Access

You can also revoke the application's access at any time:

**Facebook:** Visit Facebook Settings and remove the application's permissions under Business Integrations.

https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools

**Pinterest:** Visit your Pinterest account settings and manage or revoke connected application permissions where available.

https://www.pinterest.com/settings/

Revoking access prevents the application from making further authorized API requests but does not automatically guarantee deletion of previously stored information. You can separately request deletion by email.
